# 📚 Study Prompts

Prompts for using GPT as a study assistant.

## 1. Learn a Topic

```text
Teach me [TOPIC] as a beginner.

Please explain:
1. The basic idea
2. A simple real-world example
3. The most important concepts
4. Common mistakes
5. Give me a short practice exercise

Do not give me the answer to the exercise until I try it.
```

## 2. Create a Study Plan

```text
Create a realistic study plan for learning [TOPIC].

My current level: [BEGINNER/INTERMEDIATE/ADVANCED]
Time available each day: [TIME]
Goal: [GOAL]

Break the plan into small daily tasks and include practice exercises.
```

## 3. Quiz Me on a Topic

```text
Quiz me on [TOPIC] to test my understanding.

Please:
1. Ask me one question at a time
2. Wait for my answer before giving feedback
3. Tell me if I'm right or wrong, and explain why
4. Adjust the difficulty based on how I'm doing
5. After 5 questions, summarize what I got right and what I should review

Start with an easy question.
```

## 4. Summarize My Notes

```text
Summarize the following notes/material into a clear, organized study guide.

Please:
1. Group related ideas under short headings
2. Highlight the most important points
3. Keep the summary shorter than the original, but don't leave out key concepts
4. End with 3-5 quick review questions based on the material

Material:
[PASTE YOUR NOTES OR TEXT HERE]
```

## 5. Explain with an Analogy

```text
Explain [TOPIC] using a simple, everyday analogy so it's easier to understand.

Please:
1. Give the analogy first
2. Map each part of the analogy to the real concept
3. Point out where the analogy breaks down or isn't perfectly accurate
4. Give one more example that uses the same idea in a different context
```
